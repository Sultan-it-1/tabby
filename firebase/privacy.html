Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to Fast Toolkit. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google, we may collect:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Basic profile information provided by Google
  • Authentication information required to identify your account
  • Tool data, notes, preferences, and history that you choose to synchronize
  • AI provider credentials that you explicitly save for use with the scanning tools

We do not collect your Google password.

How We Use Information

  • Authenticate users
  • Provide access to application features
  • Maintain account security
  • Synchronize your application data between your signed-in devices
  • Improve service functionality

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. Information may only be shared when required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

Data Security

Cloud data is separated by your authenticated Firebase user ID. AI provider credentials are kept out of persistent browser local storage and are only synchronized inside your private account data. You remain responsible for rotating or revoking provider credentials if a device or account is compromised.

Data Retention

Application data may remain in Firebase while your account uses cloud synchronization. Signing out clears the active account data from the application's shared browser storage. You may contact us to request deletion of cloud data.

Third-Party Services

This application uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Sign-In, and—when selected by you—Gemini or Groq APIs. Requests sent to those services are subject to their respective privacy policies.

Your Rights

You may revoke access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account security settings.
